# MathQuest Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

MathQuest is a math practice app for children and their families, developed by @christhoval in Panama. This policy explains how the app handles information and how parents or guardians can contact the developer.

## Information stored on your device

MathQuest stores information on your device to provide its learning features:

- **Child profiles:** the name or nickname you enter, selected grade, avatar, and a locally generated profile identifier. You can use a nickname instead of a real name.
- **Learning progress:** levels, XP, coins, streaks, activity dates, daily goals, operation mastery, badges, adventure progress, and practice totals.
- **Preferences and parent settings:** language, theme, audio and haptic preferences, response mode, learning targets, screen-time limits, and usage totals used to apply those limits.

The app uses this information to show each child's progress, personalize practice, and remember your settings. MathQuest stores it in a local database and does not send profiles or learning progress to the developer. You do not need an online account to use the app.

## Advertising, tracking, and sharing

MathQuest does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting services. The developer does not sell or share child profiles or learning progress with advertisers or data brokers. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Fonts, branding images, and sound effects come bundled with the app and do not require runtime downloads.

## Children's privacy

MathQuest supports children learning with their parents or guardians. Child profile details and learning records stay on the device; the developer does not receive them through gameplay. The app includes an adult verification step for protected parent actions.

Parents and guardians should manage profiles and contact support on a child's behalf. Please avoid including a child's full name or other sensitive information in support messages.

## Support email

If you choose **Settings → Contact / Support**, MathQuest opens a draft in your email app. You review and send the message yourself. MathQuest does not automatically send your profiles or learning database with it.

If you send an email, the developer receives your email address, the message, and any attachments you choose to include. The developer uses that information to respond to your request and investigate reported problems. Your email provider also handles the message under its own privacy policy. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact [me@christhoval.dev](mailto:me@christhoval.dev).

## Storage, retention, and removal

Saved profiles, progress, and preferences remain in the app's local storage between sessions. The developer cannot access, retrieve, or erase that local data remotely.

To remove the app's local data, use your device's option to delete the app and its data, or clear app storage where your operating system supports it. Offloading an app may preserve its data. Device or operating-system backups may retain copies and may restore them later; manage those copies through your device or backup provider's settings.

Anyone who can access the app on your device may be able to view saved profiles and progress. Use your device's lock and access controls to help protect that information.

## External services and website

The MathQuest promotional website links to the App Store and offers an email support action. It does not initialize the app's local profile database or include application analytics or advertising trackers. A website hosting provider may process connection information, such as an IP address, to deliver the site.

When you open the App Store or send an email, Apple or your email provider handles information under its own policies. Your device platform may also process store, diagnostic, or backup information according to its settings and policies. Those services operate separately from MathQuest's local learning storage.

## Changes to this policy

The developer will update this policy when MathQuest's information practices change and revise the effective date above.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, parents and guardians can contact the developer, @christhoval, at [me@christhoval.dev](mailto:me@christhoval.dev).
